REGULATION (EU) 2022/2554 OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL of 14 December 2022 on digital operational resilience for the financial sector

Article 11 - General ICT business continuity policy

1. As part of their ICT risk management framework, and based on the business impact analysis, financial entities shall implement a dedicated and comprehensive ICT business continuity policy, as an integral part of the operational business continuity policy or policies of the financial entity.

2. Financial entities shall implement the ICT business continuity policy through dedicated, appropriate and documented arrangements, plans, procedures and mechanisms aiming to ensure the continuity of the financial entity's critical or important functions, the quick, appropriate and effective response to, and resolution of, all ICT-related incidents in a manner that limits damage and prioritises the resumption of activities and recovery actions.

Article 12 - Backup policies and procedures, restoration and recovery procedures and methods

1. For the purpose of ensuring the restoration of ICT systems and data with minimum downtime, limited disruption and loss, as part of their ICT business continuity policy, financial entities shall develop and document backup policies and procedures as well as restoration and recovery procedures and methods. Financial entities shall ensure that the frequency and scope of backups, and the data restoration and recovery objectives, are proportionate to their business requirements.

2. Financial entities shall implement backup policies and procedures which shall specify the scope of the data that is subject to backup and the minimum frequency of backup, based on the criticality of information or the confidentiality level of the data, as well as the critical recovery time and recovery point objectives for each system and application that is critical to the functioning of the entity.

3. Financial entities shall be able to restore backup data. They shall test backup procedures and data restoration and recovery procedures and methods regularly and at least yearly. Such tests shall apply in particular to systems and applications supporting critical or important functions, to verify that recovery can be performed within agreed timeframes. Financial entities shall retain documented evidence of the tests performed.

4. When restoring backup data, financial entities shall use segregated systems that are physically and logically separate from the source ICT system from which the backup was taken. The backup systems shall be located at a different geographical location than the primary systems to ensure resilience of operations against a physical disruptive event affecting the primary location of the financial entity.

5. Financial entities shall keep readily accessible dedicated ICT business continuity plans enabling immediate response and ensuring continuity of critical or important functions. These plans shall include a description of the critical or important functions that must be maintained and the conditions under which these plans are to be activated, and shall lay out the minimum requirements for the use of ICT capacity and the recovery timeframes.

6. Financial entities shall designate crisis management functions which, in the event of activation of their ICT business continuity plans, shall inter alia manage the internal and external communications in accordance with Article 14.

Article 13 - Learning and evolving

1. Financial entities shall have in place capabilities and staff to gather information on vulnerabilities and cyber threats, ICT-related incidents, in particular cyber-attacks, and analyse the impact they are likely to have on their digital operational resilience.

2. ICT-related incident reviews shall be conducted after a major ICT-related incident to analyse the causes of disruption and identify improvements to ICT operations or within the ICT business continuity policy.
